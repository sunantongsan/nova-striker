# Campaign and tactical orders — content 99

Replaces 20 alien missions with 100 deterministic player-building layouts, level 1–20, compartment walls and progressively introduced defenses. Previous stage/star progress is retained. Only headquarters destruction unlocks the next stage. Destroying the headquarters does not end combat, allowing a full clear.

Every attempt starts with full storage. Capacity matches the player: HQ holds 50,000 + 10,000 per additional level of each resource; shared storage holds 20,000 per level of both; dedicated storage holds 20,000 per level of its resource. Destroyed storage yields that building's contents. Destroyed HQ raises each resource's total entitlement to at least 70%, without adding a second payment; a full clear yields 100%. Rewards remain limited to free space in the attacker's own storage. No campaign NOVA reward. Existing online reward settlement is unchanged.

Select troops toggles selection mode; tap owned units to toggle selection, or select all. Tap clear ground to move, or an enemy building to focus attack. Ground routes respect intact buildings and break blocking walls for attack orders. Selecting deployment controls exits command mode. Online commands use the existing authenticated logistics channel, validate room/owner/nonce/coordinates/target, and persist in host frames for handover.

Blue-white artillery light reaches the actual 3-tile splash radius. Ground rockets use a 2.2-tile orange/yellow burst. Rocket craters remain throughout the battle and merge overlapping landing points. They clear with the scene when leaving combat.
