# Army hub and concealed defenses — patch 78

Home Army button opens the compact illustrated hub. Roster tabs scroll horizontally; tapping a portrait queues one unit, holding 320 ms starts cargo dragging. Swiping never queues production. A small info button opens stats/enhancement. Each type uses its own production duration, with an animated clock and countdown. Scroll offsets are retained after cargo moves. Cargo assignment reserves existing inventory without duplicating it.

Infantry/mechs use camps, tracked vehicles use factories, aircraft use skyports. Existing troops are preserved even if over the new category capacity; production pauses until room is available. Transport ownership is unlimited with one starter ship, 50,000 gold / 35,000 energy and 180 seconds per new ship. Capacity upgrades cost 25,000 gold / 18,000 energy times current level, taking 90 seconds times level, requiring skyport levels 3/6/9/12. Up to five loaded transports join a battle; reserves stay home. Armor is a shared NOVA enhancement capped at +10: HP 3,600 + 1,260 per step, so +10 survives four maximum-level +10 energy cannon impacts (3,600 each) and dies to the fifth without shields/healing.

Ships enter from outside and can land at any clear field location. Air support selection is saved at home. Bombers can retarget without deploying reserves; drones/helicopters enter from outside. Ground units auto-target; mechs/helicopters prefer defenses. Units route around obstacles or open a wall on their way to their goal.

Landmines and missile silos occupy one tile and are concealed from the attacking renderer until activation. Both reset in a fresh battle. HQ 3/6/9/12/15 unlocks 1/2/3/4/5 of each. Mines cost 12,000 gold / 8,000 energy and detonate once, radius 2, damage 260 + 140 per level. Silos cost 80,000 gold / 60,000 energy, detection 6.5 tiles, ammo 1/2/3 at levels 1/6/11. Each aircraft consumes one round; remaining ammo waits for another target. A hit destroys an aircraft or transport regardless of HP, with a 1% miss roll. Missiles pursue beyond detection range and map edges. Traps are excluded from destruction-percentage objectives and don't disclose their rules in player menus.

New transparent illustrated atlas, sliding silo doors, cyan projectile/impact rings, falling aircraft and temporary debris. Launch/explosion audio uses existing offline audio mixer and mute settings. Host frames retain trap ammo, reveal state, missiles, crashes and entry routes.

Full existing regression suite is also required. Real-device touch/performance validation remains outstanding.
